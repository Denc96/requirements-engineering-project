# Stakeholders

# Stakeholder needs and concerns;

## Needs:
## Student - finding and booking equipment.
## Lecturer - Getting equipment when needed for teaching.
## Equipment technician - Managing equipment and returns.
## Department admin - managing bookings and records.
## Equipment manager - Control, availability and accountability.

## Concerns:
## Student - Equipment being booked out,not being able to get it on time.
## Lecturer - Equipment not working or available.
## Equipment technician-equipment not returning, returning in a good time to check if it is still working before it goes out again.
## Department admin - bookings being double booked, records all over the place from paper to digital.
## Equipment manager - how much equipment is there for availability, is the a supplier.

# Unknowns;
## late returns or damaged - technician
## what happens if there is a double booking - department admin.
## who is the supplier - equipment manager
## security - equipment manager

# Information sources;
## Can get the sources from the stakeholders, through there documentation of bookings, supply orders.

# Elicitation questions;
## 1. Who authorises the bookings and who is allowed to book?
## 2. What happens if a booking doesn't come back or comes back passed its due date?
## 3. What would happen if the equipment is broken and is needed that day?
## 4. Who is your supplier and how often do ye contact them for supplies?
## 5. What sort of security is there for equipment to not be robbed, or damaged?
## 6. If a student and a lecturer need the equipment (the student already had it booked but the lecturer needs it for his class) at the same time, who gets priority?
